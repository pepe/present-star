# Telos: Present Star

## Who should be better off because this software exists?

Anyone who needs to present their work or knowledge.

## In what respect: what is their condition now, and what should it be after using it?

Now they have no clean way to present; after, they will have one.

## Why is that change worth it?

I can focus on the material, not on how to present it.

## Who else is affected, and does anyone lose something?

No one at the moment.

## And you: what should using and building it change for you?

I am the only user at the moment.
