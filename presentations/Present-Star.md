# Present Star
Write in Markdown. Present at a URL. Everyone follows.
---
## A deck is a Markdown file
* A frontmatter with a `title`, and an `author` and a `date` if you like
* `---` on a line of its own between slides
* Headings, bullets with `*` or `-`, and numbered lists
* **Bold**, *emphasis*, `code` and [links](https://janet-lang.org/)
---
## Code
```janet
(defn greet [who]
  (print "Hello, " who "!"))

(greet "students")
```
Fenced blocks keep their indentation, and a `---` inside them is code.

## Presenting
1. Sign in at the presenter's address
2. Press **Present** on a deck
3. → or PageDown for the next slide, ← or PageUp for the previous
---
## Following
* Students open the watcher's address
* Every slide appears there as you move
* `f` makes it full screen, for the projector
