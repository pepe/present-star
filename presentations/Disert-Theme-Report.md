# Research question

> Under what conditions can generative AI take part in the life cycle of a software system without assuming authority over its purpose, its truth, or its consequences?

- **Discipline** — Information Systems
- **Method** — Design Science Research (Hevner et al., 2004; Peffers et al., 2007)

# The gap

## The parts exist — the whole does not

- **Purpose** — elicited and critiqued, but not governed through the life cycle
- **Change** — reviewed and versioned, but AI proposals not kept apart from authority
- **Knowledge** — recorded and tested, but no sufficiency relative to consequence

Missing: one life-cycle governance design theory that integrates them

---

# Practical considerations

## Limits, obstacles, ethics

- **Researcher as designer** — explicit criteria and external review against confirmation bias
- **Few cases** — analytical, not statistical generalization
- **A moving target** — AI tools change quickly; findings are dated snapshots
- **Ethics** — GDPR, informed consent, disclosed and traceable AI use
