# Changelog
All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.
Format for entries is <version-string> - release date.

## 0.1.0 - unreleased
- Grown again as a TWM thicket: tree, decker, presenter guarded by
  presenter/sentry, watcher, and a demiurge over them.
- The lecturer presents at one URL, the students follow live at another.
- Decks in sections, titled after their files, with code blocks.
- Tables on the slides, their columns aligned as the dashes under the
  header say.
- A deck that cannot be read is refused with its line, and never cut short.
- Decks listed from the one saved last.
- A deck is staged before it is presented: the students see its title, the
  lecturer pages through it alone.
- Notes on the slides, kept by the tree for the presenter only, following
  their slides through every change of the deck.
- Thickets follow one another by public keys: a relay hands out the live
  slide to the keys it was given, and a recorder records what the relays it
  follows show, to be attended with notes and presented later.
- Styled with daisyUI, Nord and Dim as the system asks, as Interstudy is,
  slides included.
- Where a Locker admits the lecturer, the presenter's door sends them to
  it instead of asking the password, and the Locker opens the presenter in
  a window of its own: the presenter's own page, signed in.
